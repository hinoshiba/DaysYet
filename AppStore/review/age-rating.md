# Age Rating questionnaire notes

- Parental controls, age assurance, messaging, user-generated content, advertising, web browsing, gambling, loot boxes, contests: **None**
- Cartoon/fantasy violence, realistic violence, sexual content, nudity, profanity, horror/fear themes, alcohol/tobacco/drugs, weapons: **None**

Complete Apple’s current questionnaire in App Store Connect and accept the rating it calculates. Reassess whenever content, links, networking, or health features change.
