DaysYet for iPhone and iPad requires no account or sign-in and has no server-backed features.

Home Screen widgets:
1. Launch the app and complete the three-step onboarding. The milestone is optional.
2. On the Widget tab, choose three timelines, one of three layouts, and one of four themes. In Bars mode, progress fills left to right; the value can be time left, elapsed percentage, or the target date and time.
3. Add a Small or Medium DaysYet widget from the Home Screen widget gallery.
4. For per-widget overrides, touch and hold the widget, choose Edit Widget, disable “Use the three selected in the app,” and select timelines, layout, value style, and theme.

Lock Screen widgets:
1. Touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, choose Lock Screen, and tap a widget area.
2. Add “DaysYet — Lock Screen” in Inline, Circular, or Rectangular format.
3. Tap the added widget while customizing to choose one timeline. By default, it uses the first timeline selected in the app.

New in 0.1.5: Study days:
1. Open Settings > Time reference points > Choose Study Days. Set a name and start/end dates (both included, maximum 366 days); two-week and four-week shortcuts are available.
2. Select weekdays, then tap individual calendar dates to add or exclude them. Changing the weekday selection resets individual date adjustments, as stated in the editor. All weekdays can be unselected to choose dates individually. Settings save automatically.
3. Select Study days on the Widget tab or in a Home/Lock Screen widget's configuration. The remaining count includes today when selected. Progress is past selected days divided by all selected days, not completed study sessions. Empty and fully elapsed plans have distinct states. The count updates with the local date. No device calendar access or permission is used. Existing timeline selections are preserved.

Activity schedules and week starts:
1. Open Settings > Time reference points to edit dates and activities; Times lists the available timelines.
2. Activity hours 1 and Activity hours 2 each have a name, start/end times, and active weekdays. New users start with Daily activity at 07:00–23:00 every day and Work hours at 09:00–18:00 Monday–Friday. Existing users retain their previous work times, recurrence, and selections.
3. Rename a schedule, change its times, toggle weekdays On/Off, and select it in the app or a widget. All weekdays can be Off. Schedules use device local time: daytime schedules show “Not started,” time remaining, then “Finished”; inactive days show “Off.” An earlier end time ends the next day, even if that day is Off; the start day determines whether an activity runs. Equal times define 24 hours.
4. In Week starts on > First day, choose the device setting or a weekday. This week runs from local midnight on that weekday to the next occurrence, in the app and widgets.

Personal dates, goals, milestone name/dates, activity settings, study plan, week-start choice, and widget preferences stay in the on-device App Group shared with the widget extension. There is no device sync. Settings > Delete all data resets saved entries and preferences. The app makes no application-initiated network requests and includes no analytics, advertising, or third-party runtime SDKs. Public source, Privacy, and Support links open in the system browser only after a user tap.

The healthy-age goal is a user-entered planning marker, not based on a health dataset, medical advice, diagnosis, health measurement, or a lifespan prediction. Activity hours are schedule countdowns, not attendance tracking or employer reporting.
