DaysYet for iPhone and iPad needs no account or sign-in.

New in 0.2.1 (17): Optional birth-date setup, a live healthy-age countdown/percentage preview, clearer explanations, and improved large-text scrolling. The former Milestone timeline is now Target date. Existing profiles, widget choices, and colors are preserved.

Editor:
1. Onboarding explains why a birth date is used and previews the healthy-age countdown live. Choose Set up later to start without entering one; Healthy-age goal then shows Not set until saved in Times. Target dates are configured later in Times. In Widget, choose Three rows or Circles. Tap a theme preview to apply it. Circle previews offer Small, Medium, and Large. Small shows the first four circles; Medium/Large show up to eight.
2. Tap a row/circle to replace its timeline, or + to add a circle. Selecting a timeline applies immediately and returns. Back or edge swipe cancels without changing the selection. Edit reorders/removes circles; one must remain. Circles can repeat timelines. Selecting a row's existing timeline swaps the two rows.
3. Open the info button for setup guidance. About DaysYet is at the bottom of both tabs and includes privacy, licenses, version, and Delete all data.
4. Four themes are shared by rows and circles. Timeline colors opens native color pickers. Theme changes retain custom colors. In Times, touch and hold a row > Change color to customize/reset it; the accessibility action is also available. Circle-specific overrides remain intact.

Home Screen:
Touch and hold Home Screen > Edit > Add Widget > DaysYet. The primary DaysYet widget supports Small/Medium/Large and follows the app's selected rows/circles. In Edit Widget, enable Follow app appearance and set appearance options to App setting. Existing explicit three-row settings remain independent. Circles (fixed), the previous circle-only widget, remains available. App setting includes custom colors; an explicit widget theme uses that template. iOS controls refresh timing.

StandBy and Lock Screen:
Small widgets work in StandBy while charging in landscape. iOS controls background removal, rendering mode, and red Night Mode. Touch and hold Lock Screen > Customize > Lock Screen > widget area > DaysYet — Lock Screen. Inline/Circular/Rectangular forms show one timeline; tap while customizing to choose it. The default follows the first app timeline.

Times:
Tap an activity, Study days, Target date, or Healthy-age goal to edit, then Save or Cancel. Activities have names, start/end times, and weekdays. Earlier end times run overnight; equal times mean 24 hours. The start day determines whether an activity runs. This week opens the week-start setting, which also orders the study calendar without changing chosen dates.

Study days uses an inclusive range up to 366 days. Select weekdays, then add/exclude dates. Changing weekdays resets individual adjustments. Counts include today when selected; percentages measure remaining selected days, not completed sessions. No calendar access is required.

Dates, goals, schedules, and preferences remain on device, shared with the widget. Devices do not sync. Delete all data resets the saved profile and returns to onboarding. There is no application-initiated networking, analytics, advertising, or third-party runtime SDK. Public links open a browser after a tap. Healthy-age goals are personal planning markers, not medical advice or health/lifespan predictions. Activities do not track attendance or report to employers.
