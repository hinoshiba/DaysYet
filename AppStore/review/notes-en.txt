DaysYet for iPhone and iPad requires no account or sign-in and has no server-backed features.

New in 0.1.8: Four circles is a new Home Screen widget in Small and Large sizes. It shows a 2×2 grid with four independently selected timelines, defaulting to This week, This month, This year, and Daily activity. Each circle's arc shows the remaining percentage. The center value can be percentage, time left, or end date. Themes follow the app by default or can be overridden. Existing three-row and Lock Screen widgets and saved settings are preserved.

Home Screen widgets:
1. Launch the app and complete onboarding; the milestone is optional.
2. On the Widget tab, choose three timelines, one of three layouts, and one of four themes for the existing three-row widget. Add it in Small or Medium. For overrides, touch and hold > Edit Widget, disable Use the three selected in the app, and choose timelines, layout, values, and theme.
3. For the new widget, choose Four circles in the gallery and add Small or Large. Touch and hold > Edit Widget to set Top-left, Top-right, Bottom-left, and Bottom-right timeline, Value style, and Theme. Its four choices are separate from the app's three timelines. Values use compact text; VoiceOver reads each full name and complete time details. Refresh timing is controlled by iOS. Calendar periods and default activities work before onboarding; personal goals are replaced with available periods until configured.

Lock Screen widgets:
Touch and hold the Lock Screen > Customize > Lock Screen > tap a widget area. Add DaysYet — Lock Screen in Inline, Circular, or Rectangular form. Tap it while customizing to choose one timeline; the default follows the first timeline selected in the app.

Time settings:
On the Times tab, tap Daily activity, Work hours, Study days, Milestone, or Healthy-age goal to edit its own settings. These cards show a chevron; This week, This month, and This year have no settings and are not tappable. Settings retains Week starts on, shared by This week and the study calendar.

Study days:
Tap Study days on Times. Set a name and inclusive date range up to 366 days; two/four-week shortcuts are available. Select weekdays, then add/exclude individual dates. Changing weekdays resets individual adjustments, as stated in the editor; all weekdays can be unselected to choose dates individually. Changes save automatically. Select Study days in the app or any widget. Counts include today when selected. The percentage is selected days still available divided by all selected days, not completed study sessions. Empty and elapsed plans have distinct states. Local dates are used without device-calendar access or permission.

Activity schedules and week starts:
Each activity has a name, start/end times, and active weekdays. Defaults are Daily activity 07:00–23:00 daily and Work hours 09:00–18:00 Monday–Friday. Edit on Times, then select the activity in the app or widget. All weekdays can be Off. Schedules use device local time and show Not started, time left, Finished, or Off. Earlier end times finish the next day even if Off; the start day determines whether it runs. Equal times mean 24 hours. In Settings > Week starts on > First day, choose the device setting or a weekday. This week runs from local midnight to the next occurrence.

Dates, goals, schedules, and preferences remain on device, shared with the widget extension; there is no device sync. Settings > Delete all data resets them. No application-initiated networking, analytics, ads, or third-party runtime SDKs. Public links open in a browser after a tap. Healthy-age goals are user-entered planning markers, not medical advice, health measurement, or health/lifespan predictions. Activities do not track attendance or report to employers.
