DaysYet for iPhone and iPad needs no account or sign-in.

New in 0.1.9: Three rows and Four circles share an editor with saved timelines, values, themes, and colors. Names sit inside rings above values; long names truncate visually but VoiceOver reads them in full. Text and tracks adapt when iOS removes widget backgrounds. Build 14 also aligns Times and Settings titles/backgrounds with Widget. At accessibility text sizes, time cards stack titles above values and wrap text. Navigation and saved settings are unchanged.

Editor and colors:
1. Complete onboarding; the milestone is optional. On Widget, select Three rows or Four circles: preview, timelines, then display settings. Settings > Widget settings opens the same tab. This selector does not change a placed widget's type.
2. Colors contains the preview, four templates, and native pickers for visible timelines. Changes save immediately. Reset controls appear for custom colors. Selecting a template clears that design's individual colors, including hidden timelines.
3. Row/circle colors save separately. Circles can follow the row palette/colors with their own overrides or use an independent template. Repeated circle timelines share one color. Pickers allow arbitrary opaque colors.

Home Screen widgets:
Touch and hold the Home Screen > Edit > Add Widget > DaysYet. Three rows supports Small/Medium; Four circles supports Small/Large. Each colored arc shows the remaining percentage; center values can be percentage, time left, or end date.

New circle widgets follow app settings. For an existing widget, touch and hold > Edit Widget and set each position, Value style, and Theme to App setting. Explicit choices remain available. Defaults: This week/month/year, Daily activity, Remaining percentage, and the Three rows palette. A center value already using App setting now follows the dedicated circle value instead of the row value.

For Three rows, Edit Widget > enable Use the three selected in the app to follow app timelines; choose App setting for layout, values, and theme. Disable the switch for separate timelines. For either design, Theme = App setting includes the app's custom colors; an explicit theme uses its unmodified template. iOS controls refresh timing. Toolbar help covers installation and applying changes.

StandBy and Lock Screen:
Small widgets work in StandBy while charging in landscape. iOS controls background removal, rendering mode, and red Night Mode; the app cannot force full color or translucency. Touch and hold the Lock Screen > Customize > Lock Screen > widget area > DaysYet — Lock Screen. Inline, Circular, and Rectangular forms show one timeline. Tap while customizing to select it; the default follows the first app timeline.

Time settings:
On Times, tap Daily activity, Work hours, Study days, Milestone, or Healthy-age goal to edit. This week/month/year have no individual settings. Activities have names, start/end times, and active weekdays. Earlier end times run overnight; equal times mean 24 hours, and the starting day controls activity. Settings > Week starts on chooses the device setting or a weekday.

Study days:
Set an inclusive range up to 366 days, select weekdays, then add/exclude dates. Changing weekdays resets individual adjustments. Counts include today when selected; percentages measure remaining selected days, not completed sessions. No calendar access or permission is required.

Dates, goals, schedules, and preferences stay on device, shared with the widget extension; there is no device sync. Settings > Delete all data resets them. No application-initiated networking, analytics, ads, or third-party runtime SDKs. Public links open a browser after a tap. Healthy-age goals are personal planning markers, not medical advice, health measurements, or health/lifespan predictions. Activities do not track attendance or report to employers.
