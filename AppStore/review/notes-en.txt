DaysYet for iPhone and iPad needs no account or sign-in.

New in 0.1.10 (15): Balanced Four circles with one-line names, consistent percentages, and proportionate rings. VoiceOver retains full names. Small shows the leading duration unit, two-digit years, and 24-hour activity times. Settings returns to the type chooser and remembers the last editor. Colors & palette and add/switch guidance are below the Small preview. Times cards have a Change color shortcut. Saved settings, overrides, and colors are preserved.

Editor and colors:
1. Complete onboarding; the milestone is optional. On Widget, choose Three timelines or Four circles. Settings > Widget settings returns to this chooser, even from Colors & palette. This edits settings; it does not replace a placed widget.
2. Colors & palette below the preview has four templates and native color pickers. Changes save immediately. Custom colors have reset controls. Choosing a template clears that design's individual colors, including hidden timelines.
3. On Times, touch and hold a card > Change color to open its native color picker and optional reset. An accessibility action is also available. Cards use the same colors as Three timelines; normal taps still edit schedules/dates where available.
4. Row/circle colors save separately. Circles can follow the Three timelines palette with overrides or use an independent template. Repeated timelines share a color. Pickers allow arbitrary opaque colors.

Home Screen:
Add or switch on the Home Screen opens instructions. Touch and hold the Home Screen > Edit > Add Widget > DaysYet. Choose DaysYet — Three timelines (Small/Medium) or Four circles (Small/Large). To switch kinds, add the desired widget, then optionally remove the old one. Arcs show remaining percentage; center values show percentage, time left, or end date.

New circles follow app settings. For existing circles, touch and hold > Edit Widget and set positions, Value style, and Theme to App setting. Explicit choices remain independent. Defaults: This week/month/year, Daily activity, Remaining percentage, and the Three timelines palette.

For Three timelines, enable Use the three selected in the app and choose App setting for display mode, values, and theme. Disable that switch for separate timelines. Theme = App setting includes custom colors; an explicit theme uses its template. iOS controls refresh timing.

StandBy and Lock Screen:
Small widgets work in StandBy while charging in landscape. iOS controls background removal, rendering mode, and red Night Mode; the app cannot force full color or translucency. Touch and hold the Lock Screen > Customize > Lock Screen > widget area > DaysYet — Lock Screen. Inline, Circular, and Rectangular forms show one timeline. Tap while customizing to select it; the default follows the first app timeline.

Time settings:
On Times, tap Daily activity, Work hours, Study days, Milestone, or Healthy-age goal to edit. Activities have names, start/end times, and weekdays. Earlier end times run overnight; equal times mean 24 hours. The starting day controls activity. Settings > Week starts on chooses the device setting or a weekday.

Study days:
Set an inclusive range up to 366 days, select weekdays, then add/exclude dates. Changing weekdays resets individual adjustments. Counts include today when selected; percentages measure remaining selected days, not completed sessions. No calendar access is required.

Dates, goals, schedules, and timeline preferences stay on device, shared with the widget; the last editor choice is app-only. No device sync. Settings > Delete all data resets both. No application-initiated networking, analytics, ads, or third-party runtime SDKs. Public links open a browser after a tap. Healthy-age goals are planning markers, not medical advice, health measurements, or health/lifespan predictions. Activities do not track attendance or report to employers.
