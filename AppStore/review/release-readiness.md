## Screenshot acceptance

Apple accepts 1–10 screenshots per applicable set, without alpha channels. Mac screenshots use a 16:10 aspect ratio at 1280 × 800, 1440 × 900, 2560 × 1600, or 2880 × 1800. See the current [screenshot specifications](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/screenshot-specifications/). Passing a size check does not prove that the screenshot shows the submitted version.

## Submission fields

Keep App Review contact information complete and current in App Store Connect; Apple describes it as review information that is not visible to customers. See [platform version information](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/platform-version-information/). No login account is needed for DaysYet. The upload, **Add for Review**, and **Submit for Review** actions are separate; see [Submit an app](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-submissions-to-app-review/submit-an-app).
