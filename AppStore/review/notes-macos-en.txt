DaysYet is a native macOS 14+ app. No account or sign-in is required. It is not a WidgetKit extension or added through the macOS widget gallery.

New in 0.1.6:
In Settings > Widget, choose Left or Right. Under Graph magnification, enable Enlarge the graph under the pointer (off by default). Set Magnification to 110–180% (135% default). Hover a circle and move between circles: the hovered circle grows and shifts toward the screen center as details open or move in sync. Moving away shrinks the circle and closes unpinned details. Top placement has no graph magnification. Set Detail size to 100–150% (100% default), independently of Widget size, at any edge. With macOS Reduce Motion enabled, these changes have no animation.

Resident panel and customization:
1. Launch DaysYet: three right-edge circles show this week, month, and year without personal dates. Hover for details. Double-click the panel, circle, or detail surface for settings; single-click does not open settings. There is no menu bar item or custom right-click menu.
2. In Settings > Timelines > Your timelines, Add Circle selects 3–8 distinct timelines; remove controls appear above three. Selecting an already displayed timeline swaps positions. Left/right shows all circles; top shows only the first three and saves later selections.
3. Drag a side panel vertically from its surface, circle, or detail. Release saves its position for relaunch. Dragging holds details and does not open settings. Settings offer Center, not a vertical slider. Top placement stays centered and does not drag. Settings > Widget selects display, edge, and size (80–150%).
4. Widget > Colors offers four templates and per-timeline colors, saved immediately. Reset one or all colors to the template; choosing a template replaces custom colors. Top shows color controls for the first three timelines.
5. Enable Launch at login in Widget to start at sign-in. If macOS approval is needed, use Open Login Items Settings, enable it there, then return to refresh status.
6. Detail modes: Countdown, Time + elapsed % + bar, or Progress bars. Progress bars can show time left, elapsed progress, or end date. Hover a top bar or just above it to select a timeline; higher in the camera area opens the current one. Moving away closes details unless Keep details open is enabled. Settings offer Show/hide and Quit. Relaunching a hidden app opens settings to restore visibility. Closing settings leaves the panel running.

Study days:
Open Settings > Timelines > Choose Study Days. Set a name and inclusive range of up to 366 days (two/four-week shortcuts available). Choose weekdays, then add/exclude calendar dates. Changing weekdays resets individual date adjustments; all weekdays can be deselected to choose dates individually. Changes save automatically. Select Study days under Your timelines. Remaining days include today when selected; progress is past selected days divided by all selected days, not completed study tracking. Empty and elapsed plans have distinct states. Counts use the local date without Mac calendar access or permission.

Timelines also has two named activity schedules with start/end times and weekdays. Defaults: Daily activity 07:00–23:00 daily; Work hours 09:00–18:00 Monday–Friday. Earlier end times finish next day even if Off; the start day determines whether it runs. Equal times mean 24 hours; all days can be Off. Existing work times and selections are preserved. Week starts on uses the device calendar or a chosen weekday.

Dates, goals, activities, study plans, and preferences stay in this Mac's sandbox, with no iPhone/iPad sync. Delete all data resets them. No application-initiated network requests, analytics, ads, or third-party runtime SDKs. Public links open a browser after user action. Healthy-age goals are planning markers, not medical advice or health/lifespan predictions. Activities do not track attendance or report to employers.
