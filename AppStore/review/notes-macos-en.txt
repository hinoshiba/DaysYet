DaysYet is a native macOS 14+ app. No account or sign-in is required. It is a resident app, not a WidgetKit extension.

New in 0.1.7:
At the left and right edges, the pointer moving onto an enlarged circle's details keeps that circle magnified and the details open, including across the gap between them. Moving to another timeline's details moves the selection and magnification there. Leaving the widget restores the size and closes unpinned details; with Keep details open, returning re-magnifies the selected circle. Top placement is unchanged.

Magnification and detail size:
In Settings > Widget, choose Left or Right, enable Enlarge the graph under the pointer (off by default), and set Magnification to 110–180% (135% default). Top placement has no graph magnification. Set Detail size to 100–150% (100% default), independently of Widget size, at any edge. With Reduce Motion enabled, these changes have no animation.

Resident panel and customization:
1. Launch DaysYet: three right-edge circles show this week, month, and year without personal dates. Hover for details. Double-click the panel, circle, or detail for settings; single-click does not. There is no menu bar item or custom right-click menu.
2. In Settings > Timelines > Your timelines, Add Circle selects 3–8 distinct timelines; remove controls appear above three. Left/right shows all circles; top shows only the first three and saves later selections.
3. Drag a side panel vertically from its surface, circle, or detail. Release saves its position for relaunch. Dragging holds details and does not open settings. Settings offer Center, not a vertical slider. Top placement stays centered and does not drag. Settings > Widget selects display, edge, and size (80–150%).
4. Widget > Colors offers four templates and per-timeline colors, saved immediately. Reset one or all colors to the template; choosing a template replaces custom colors. Top shows color controls for the first three timelines.
5. Enable Launch at login in Widget to start at sign-in. If macOS approval is needed, use Open Login Items Settings, enable it there, then return to refresh status.
6. Detail modes: Countdown, Time + remaining % + bar, or Progress bars. Progress bars show time left, remaining percentage, or end date. Hover a top bar or just above it to select a timeline; higher in the camera area opens the current one. Settings offer Show/hide and Quit; relaunch opens settings if hidden. Closing settings leaves the panel running.

Study days:
Open Settings > Timelines > Choose Study Days. Set a name and inclusive range of up to 366 days (two/four-week shortcuts available). Choose weekdays, then add/exclude calendar dates. Changing weekdays resets individual date adjustments; all weekdays can be deselected to choose dates individually. Changes save automatically. Select Study days under Your timelines. Remaining days include today when selected; the remaining percentage is selected days still available (including today when selected) divided by all selected days, not completed study tracking. Empty and elapsed plans have distinct states. Counts use local dates without calendar access.

Timelines also has two named activity schedules with start/end times and weekdays. Defaults: Daily activity 07:00–23:00 daily; Work hours 09:00–18:00 Monday–Friday. Earlier end times finish next day even if Off; the start day determines whether it runs. Equal times mean 24 hours; all days can be Off. Existing work times and selections are preserved. Week starts on uses the device calendar or a chosen weekday.

Dates, goals, activities, study plans, and preferences stay in this Mac's sandbox, with no iPhone/iPad sync. Delete all data resets them. No application-initiated network requests, analytics, ads, or third-party runtime SDKs. Public links open a browser after user action. Healthy-age goals are planning markers, not medical advice or health/lifespan predictions. Activities do not track attendance or report to employers.
