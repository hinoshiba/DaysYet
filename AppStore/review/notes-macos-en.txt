DaysYet for Mac is a native desktop app for macOS 14 or later. It requires no account or sign-in. It is not a WidgetKit extension and is not added through the macOS Widget gallery.

1. Launch DaysYet. Three progress circles appear at the right edge of the display for this week, this month, and this year. No dates are required for these defaults.
2. Hover over a circle to reveal details beside it. The expanded section follows the selected circle. Double-click the widget body, circle, or detail surface to open settings. A single click does not open settings. There is no menu bar item or custom right-click menu.
3. Drag anywhere on the left or right widget's black surface, including a circle or open detail, to move it vertically within its display. Release to save the position, which is restored when the app reopens. While dragging, the current open/closed state is held; normal hover resumes on release. A drag does not open settings. Top placement stays centered and does not drag. Settings also let you choose three timelines, four themes, a display, left/right/top placement, vertical position for side placement, and size from 80–150%. Detail modes are Countdown, Time + elapsed % + bar, and Progress bars. In Progress bars mode, choose time left, elapsed progress, or the end date as the value.
4. At the top, three thin bars sit below the physical camera area, or at the top center of a display without a camera cutout. Hover over a bar or directly above it to select a timeline. Higher in the camera area, hovering opens the current timeline.
5. Move the pointer away to close details. “Keep details open” is available in settings; turn it off to close a kept-open detail view. Show/hide and Quit are also in settings. If hidden, launch DaysYet again to reopen settings and restore visibility. Closing settings leaves the widget running.
6. Under Timelines, set Work hours and Week starts on. Work hours defaults to 09:00–18:00 and repeats daily in local time. A daytime shift shows “Not started,” remaining time, then “Work finished.” Earlier end times finish the next day; equal times define 24 hours. Week starts can follow the device calendar or an explicit weekday, from local midnight to the next occurrence.

Personal dates are optional. Profile entries, work times, week-start choice, and panel preferences, including the dragged vertical position, are stored only in this Mac app’s sandbox. They are separate from the iPhone/iPad App Group, with no cross-device sync. Delete all data also resets panel placement and size. The app makes no application-initiated network requests and includes no analytics, advertising, or third-party runtime SDKs. Public links open in the system browser after a user action.

The healthy-age goal is a user-chosen planning marker, not medical advice or a health/lifespan prediction. Work hours is a daily countdown, not attendance tracking or employer reporting.
