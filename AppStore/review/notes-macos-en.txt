DaysYet is a native macOS 14+ resident app, not a WidgetKit extension. No account or sign-in is required.

New in 0.1.8:
With graph magnification enabled at the left/right edge, circle enlargement/shrinking and detail opening/closing use the same progress. The circle no longer shrinks before its details close. Returning during closing reopens smoothly from the current size. Moving onto details keeps the circle magnified. Clicks and double-clicks preserve magnification; a drag restores the normal size only after movement passes the drag threshold. Double-click still opens settings. Saved dates, schedules, and display settings are preserved.

Magnification and detail size:
In Settings > Widget, choose Left or Right, enable Enlarge the graph under the pointer (off by default), and set Magnification to 110–180% (135% default). Top has no graph magnification. Detail size is 100–150% (100% default), independent of Widget size, at any edge. Reduce Motion switches without animation.

Resident panel and customization:
1. Launch DaysYet: three right-edge circles show this week/month/year without personal dates. Hover for details. Double-click the panel, circle, or detail for settings; single-click does not open them. No menu bar item or custom right-click menu.
2. Settings > Timelines > Your timelines allows 3–8 distinct timelines. Remove controls appear above three. Left/right shows all circles; top shows the first three and retains the rest.
3. Drag a side panel vertically from its surface, circle, or detail. Releasing saves the position for relaunch. Dragging holds details and does not open settings. Settings offers Center, not a vertical slider. Top stays centered and does not drag. Settings > Widget selects display, edge, and size (80–150%).
4. Widget > Colors offers four templates and per-timeline colors, saved immediately. Reset one/all to the template; choosing a template replaces custom colors. Top shows controls for the first three timelines.
5. Enable Launch at login in Widget. If approval is needed, Open Login Items Settings, enable it there, then return to refresh status.
6. Detail modes are Countdown, Time + remaining % + bar, or Progress bars. Bars show time left, remaining percentage, or end date. Hover a top bar or just above it to select a timeline; higher in the camera area opens the current one. Show/hide and Quit are in settings; relaunch opens settings when hidden. Closing settings leaves the panel running.

Study days:
Settings > Timelines > Choose Study Days sets a name and inclusive range up to 366 days, with two/four-week shortcuts. Choose weekdays, then add/exclude calendar dates. Changing weekdays resets individual adjustments; all weekdays can be deselected to choose dates individually. Changes save automatically. Select Study days under Your timelines. Remaining days include today when selected; percentage is selected days still available divided by all selected days, not completed study tracking. Empty and elapsed plans have distinct states. Local dates are used without calendar access.

Timelines has two named activity schedules with start/end times and weekdays. Defaults: Daily activity 07:00–23:00 daily; Work hours 09:00–18:00 Monday–Friday. Earlier end times finish next day even if Off; the start day determines whether it runs. Equal times mean 24 hours; all days can be Off. Week starts on uses the device calendar or a chosen weekday.

Dates, goals, activities, study plans, and preferences remain in this Mac's sandbox, without iPhone/iPad sync. Delete all data resets them. No application-initiated networking, analytics, ads, or third-party runtime SDKs. Public links open in a browser after user action. Healthy-age goals are planning markers, not medical advice or health/lifespan predictions. Activities do not track attendance or report to employers.
