DaysYet is a sandboxed native Mac app, sharing the existing DaysYet App Store record. No account, sign-in, subscription, or purchase is needed. It supports macOS 14+, Japanese and English, Apple silicon and Intel.

New in 0.2.0 (13): Magnetic dragging between screen edges, bottom-edge placement, first-run placement/movement guidance, and improved pointer and display handling. This App Store build uses screen-edge widgets. It does not place timeline icons inside the system Dock and does not request Accessibility or Screen Recording access.

Review steps:
1. Launch and follow the first-run guide. Launch at login is optional. A small black widget sits at a screen edge. Hover a circle to open its remaining time and percentage. Double-click to open settings; closing settings leaves the resident widget running.
2. Grab a circle and drag. The group gathers into that circle; approaching an available screen edge previews the new arrangement. Release near the edge to save. Release away from an edge or press Esc to restore the original position. The edge occupied by the macOS Dock is unavailable.
3. Widget > Screen edge also selects left/right/top/bottom. Choose a display. Top shows the first three timelines as bars; sides and bottom show the configured circles. Bottom expands upward. Existing preferences are preserved, with unavailable Dock-edge placement returning to an available edge.
4. Adjust widget size (80–150%) and Detail size (100–150%). Choose Countdown, Time + remaining % + bar, or Progress bars. Bars can show time left, percentage, or end date. Keep details open is optional. Graph magnification is off by default; when enabled, hovered circles enlarge by 110–180%. Reduce Motion skips animation.
5. In Timelines, choose three to eight different timelines. Selecting a timeline already used swaps positions. Top displays only the first three while preserving the rest. Four color templates and individual native color pickers are in Widget. Custom colors can be reset.
6. Show/hide, optional Launch at login, and Quit are available in settings. Relaunching a hidden widget opens settings. Privacy & About includes public policy/license links and Delete all data.

Timelines:
Two activity schedules have names, start/end times, and active weekdays. Overnight activities continue until their end even if the following day is Off; equal times mean 24 hours. The starting day determines whether the activity runs. Week starts on follows the device calendar or a chosen weekday.

Choose Study Days opens a name and inclusive range of up to 366 days, with two/four-week shortcuts. Select weekdays, then add/exclude calendar dates. Changing weekdays resets individual adjustments. Changes save automatically. Counts include today when selected; percentages measure remaining selected days, not completed study sessions. No device-calendar access is required.

Dates, goals, activities, study plans, and preferences stay in this Mac's sandbox, without device sync. Delete all data resets them. No application-initiated networking, analytics, ads, or third-party runtime SDKs. Public links open a browser after user action. Healthy-age goals are personal planning markers, not medical advice or health/lifespan predictions. Activities do not track attendance or report to employers.
