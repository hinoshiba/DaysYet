Make time for what matters. DaysYet brings life and everyday countdowns to your Home and Lock Screens. See time left for your week, month, year, activities, workday, study days, and personal milestones.

Set your own healthy-age goal as a personal planning milestone. DaysYet counts down to the age you choose; it does not predict your health or lifespan.

THREE TIMELINES
Choose three from this week, month, year, two activity schedules, your healthy-age goal, a milestone date with its own start date, and study days. Use the app's selection or a different combination for each widget.

FOUR CIRCLES
Show four timelines in a 2×2 grid with Small and Large Home Screen widgets. One-line names sit above values inside each ring; long names truncate visually. Percentage digits keep a consistent size. Set each position, center value (percentage, time left, or end date), and theme in the app. Small shows the leading duration unit, dates with two-digit years, and activity times in 24-hour format. Large and VoiceOver retain more detail. Circle settings are separate from Three timelines; widgets can override them.

FIND YOUR WIDGET SETTINGS
On Widget, choose Three timelines or Four circles and preview changes. Settings > Widget settings returns to the type chooser. The app remembers your last editor selection. The circle preview shows the Small layout.

Colors & palette is directly below the preview. Add or switch on the Home Screen explains installation. The type selector chooses what to edit; it does not replace a placed widget. Add the desired type from the system gallery. New circle widgets follow app settings. To apply them to an existing widget, touch and hold > Edit Widget and choose App setting.

TWO ACTIVITY SCHEDULES AND YOUR WEEK
Set custom names, start/end times, and active weekdays. Overnight activities use device-local time and continue until their end even if the next day is Off. Choose the first day of This week from the device setting or any weekday. Tap an activity on Times to edit it.

COUNT YOUR AVAILABLE STUDY DAYS
Name a plan of up to 366 days, select weekdays, and add or exclude dates. Two-week and four-week shortcuts help you start. Counts include today when selected. Percentages measure remaining selected days, not completed sessions. No device-calendar access is needed. Tap Study days on Times to edit.

THREE DISPLAY LAYOUTS
Choose a large countdown, a combined time/percentage/bar view, or three progress bars. Color shows time remaining, decreasing from 100% to 0%. Bars shrink from right to left; rings drain clockwise from the top. Bar values can show time left, percentage, or the end date.

YOUR COLORS
Colors & palette offers four templates and native color pickers. On Times, touch and hold a card > Change color to customize or reset its color. Cards share colors with Three timelines. Row and circle colors save separately; circles can follow the Three timelines palette. A widget's App setting theme uses your colors; an explicit theme uses its template.

HOME SCREEN, LOCK SCREEN, AND STANDBY
Three timelines supports Small and Medium; Four circles supports Small and Large. Inline, Circular, and Rectangular Lock Screen widgets show one timeline. Small widgets also work in StandBy, adapting text when the background is removed. iOS controls background and color rendering.

JAPANESE AND ENGLISH
Use the app, widgets, and widget configuration in Japanese or English.

ON DEVICE BY DESIGN
No account is required. Dates, goals, schedules, and preferences stay on device and are never sent to the developer. Timeline settings are shared with the widget; the last editor choice stays in the app. No advertising or analytics SDKs. Delete all saved data from Settings at any time.

Healthy-age goals are personal planning markers, not statistics, medical advice, diagnoses, or health/lifespan predictions.
