Make time for what matters. DaysYet brings life and everyday countdowns to your Home and Lock Screens. See time left for your week, month, year, activities, workday, study days, and personal milestones.

Set your own healthy-age goal as a personal planning milestone. DaysYet counts down to the age you choose; it does not predict your health or lifespan.

THREE TIMELINES
Choose three from this week, month, year, two activity schedules, your healthy-age goal, a milestone date with its own start date, and study days. Use the app's selection or a different combination for each widget.

CIRCLES
Choose one to eight timelines. Small shows the first four; Medium and Large show up to eight. Names sit above values inside each ring, and percentage digits keep a consistent size. Choose percentage, time left, or end date for the center. Small and dense layouts use compact values; VoiceOver retains full detail.

WIDGET AND TIMES
On Widget, choose Three rows or Circles. The installed DaysYet widget follows your layout, timelines, and theme. Tap one of four live theme previews to select it. Circle previews offer Small, Medium, and Large. Use + to add from Times, tap to replace, and Edit to reorder or remove. Schedules and dates live in Times, with Save and Cancel.

The info button opens a guide from the bottom. Timeline selection opens a separate screen with a Back button. About DaysYet, at the bottom of either tab, contains privacy, license, and data-reset controls. Existing custom three-row widgets keep their choices. The original circle-only widget remains as Circles (fixed). To follow the app, add DaysYet; in Edit Widget, enable Follow app appearance and choose App setting for appearance options.

TWO ACTIVITY SCHEDULES AND YOUR WEEK
Set names, start/end times, and active weekdays for two schedules. Overnight activities use device-local time and continue until their end even if the next day is Off. Tap a schedule in Times to edit. Tap This week to choose its first day; this also orders the study calendar without changing selected dates.

COUNT YOUR AVAILABLE STUDY DAYS
Name a plan of up to 366 days, select weekdays, and add or exclude dates. Two-week and four-week shortcuts help you start. Counts include today when selected. Percentages measure remaining selected days, not completed sessions. No device-calendar access is needed. Tap Study days on Times to edit.

THREE DISPLAY LAYOUTS
Choose a large countdown, a combined time/percentage/bar view, or three progress bars. Color shows time remaining, decreasing from 100% to 0%. Bars shrink from right to left; rings drain clockwise from the top. Bar values can show time left, percentage, or the end date.

YOUR COLORS
Four themes are shared by rows and circles. Timeline colors offers native color pickers. In Times, touch and hold a row > Change color to customize or reset it. Circles inherit these colors, keeping any circle-specific overrides. A widget's App setting theme uses your colors; an explicit widget theme uses its template.

HOME SCREEN, LOCK SCREEN, AND STANDBY
Rows and circles support Small, Medium, and Large. Inline, Circular, and Rectangular Lock Screen widgets show one timeline. Small widgets also work in StandBy, adapting text when the background is removed. iOS controls background and color rendering.

JAPANESE AND ENGLISH
Use the app, widgets, and widget configuration in Japanese or English.

ON DEVICE BY DESIGN
No account is required. Dates, goals, schedules, and preferences stay on device and are never sent to the developer. No advertising or analytics SDKs. Delete all saved data from About DaysYet at the bottom of either tab.

Healthy-age goals are personal planning markers, not statistics, medical advice, diagnoses, or health/lifespan predictions.
