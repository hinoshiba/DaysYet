Make time for what matters. DaysYet brings life and everyday countdowns to your Home and Lock Screens. See time left and the percentage remaining for your week, month, year, daily activities, workday, study days, and personal milestones.

Set your own healthy-age goal as a personal planning milestone. DaysYet counts down to the age you choose; it does not predict your health or lifespan.

Keep three timeline rows or four circles on your Home Screen, and one timeline on your Lock Screen.

THREE TIMELINES AT A GLANCE
Choose from this week, this month, this year, two activity schedules, a healthy-age goal you set, a milestone date with its own start date, and study days. Use the three selected in the app, or configure a different combination for each widget.

FOUR CIRCLES
Show four timelines in a 2×2 grid with Small and Large Home Screen widgets. Each name sits inside its ring, in smaller text above the value. Defaults are This week, This month, This year, and Daily activity. Configure each position, percentage/time left/end date for the center, and a theme in the app. Circle settings are saved separately from Three rows, and individual widgets can override them.

ONE PLACE TO EDIT WIDGETS
Switch between Three rows and Four circles on the Widget tab. Preview each design and edit its timelines, values, and colors in the same order. Settings > Widget settings opens the same editor. New circle widgets use the app settings. To apply them to an existing widget, touch and hold it > Edit Widget and set its fields to App setting. The toolbar help explains how to add widgets and apply changes.

TWO ACTIVITY SCHEDULES AND YOUR WEEK
Set up two activities with custom names, start/end times, and active weekdays. Use overnight schedules in your device’s local time; an activity continues until its end even if the next day is Off. Choose the first day of This week using the device setting or any weekday. Tap an activity on Times to edit it.

COUNT YOUR AVAILABLE STUDY DAYS
Name a plan of up to 366 days, select weekdays, and add or exclude individual dates. Two-week and four-week shortcuts help you start. Show remaining selected days in the app or widgets, including today when selected. The percentage measures days still available, not completed study sessions. No device-calendar access is needed. Tap Study days on Times to edit.

THREE DISPLAY LAYOUTS
Switch between a large countdown, a combined time/percentage/bar view, and three progress bars. Color shows time remaining, decreasing from 100% to 0%. Bars shrink from right to left; rings drain clockwise from the top. For progress bars, choose time left, remaining percentage, or the target date and time.

YOUR COLORS, YOUR WAY
Open Colors for a preview, four palette templates, and a color picker for each visible timeline. Choose your own colors and reset changes when needed. Row and circle colors are saved separately; circles can follow rows. A widget's App setting theme uses your colors; an explicit theme uses its template.

Three-row widgets support Small and Medium; Four circles supports Small and Large. Inline, Circular, and Rectangular Lock Screen widgets are also available. Choose one timeline for the Lock Screen. Small widgets also work in StandBy, with readable text when the background is removed. iOS controls background and color rendering.

JAPANESE AND ENGLISH
Use the app, widget, and widget configuration in Japanese or English.

ON DEVICE BY DESIGN
No account is required. Your birth date, configured dates, activity names, times and weekdays, week-start choice, and study plan remain in the on-device area shared by the app and widget and are not sent to the developer. DaysYet contains no advertising or analytics SDKs. You can delete all saved data from Settings at any time.

The healthy-age goal is a personal planning marker you choose. It is not a statistic, medical advice, diagnosis, or a prediction of your health or lifespan.
