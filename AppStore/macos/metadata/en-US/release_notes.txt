• Add optional graph magnification for left and right placement. Turn on Enlarge the graph under the pointer in Settings > Widget and choose 110–180% magnification (135% by default). The option is off by default.
• Circles move slightly toward the screen center as they grow to stay within the edge. Enlargement and detail opening, closing, and movement start together; detail text grows with the panel.
• Adjust Detail size to 100–150% (100% by default), independently of the overall widget size. Available at the left, right, and top edges.
• With Reduce Motion enabled in macOS, magnification and details change without animation.
