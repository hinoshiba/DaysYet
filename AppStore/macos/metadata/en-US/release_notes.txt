• At the left and right edges, moving the pointer onto an enlarged circle’s details now keeps that circle magnified and its details open, including while crossing the gap between them.
• Moving to another timeline’s details moves the selection and the magnification to that circle. Leaving the widget restores the circle’s size and closes details that are not pinned.
• With Keep details open selected, returning to the details magnifies the selected circle again. Top placement is unchanged.
