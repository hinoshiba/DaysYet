Make time for what matters. DaysYet keeps three life and everyday countdowns at the edge of your Mac display. See time left and progress for your week, month, year, workday and personal milestones while you work.

Use it as a quiet memento mori: a reminder to be intentional with your time. Set your own healthy-age goal as a personal planning milestone. DaysYet counts down to the age you choose; it does not predict your health or lifespan.

THREE TIMELINES AT THE EDGE
Choose small progress circles on the left or right, or three thin bars at the top. Hover to expand the black panel and reveal the selected timeline. On either side, the details move up or down to follow the circle you select.

FIT YOUR WORKSPACE
Choose left, right, or top placement and the display to use. Drag a side widget up or down; DaysYet saves its position. Adjust the size from 80–150% and choose from four color palettes. Double-click to open settings, where you can also show, hide, or quit the app.

CHOOSE YOUR LEVEL OF DETAIL
Pick a large time-left countdown, a combined view of time left, elapsed percentage, and a bar, or a progress-bar view. In the progress-bar view, choose time left, elapsed percentage, or the end date as the value. Details close when you move away, or you can keep them open from settings.

TWO ACTIVITY SCHEDULES AND YOUR WEEK
Set up two activities, such as your whole day and work hours, with custom names, start and end times, and active weekdays. Set individual weekdays to Off and use overnight schedules in your Mac's local time. An activity that has started continues until its end, even if the next day is Off. Start This week on the day set by your Mac calendar or choose any weekday. Edit these settings in the Timelines tab. Your existing work times and display selections are preserved.

IMPORTANT DATES AND PERSONAL GOALS
Choose three timelines from this week, this month, this year, two activity schedules, a milestone with its own start date, and a healthy-age goal you set. The default week, month, and year work without entering any personal dates. Japanese and English are supported.

STORED ON THIS MAC
No account is required. Dates, activity names, times and weekdays, week-start choices, and display settings stay on your Mac and are not sent to the developer. DaysYet has no advertising or analytics SDKs. There is no data sync with iPhone or iPad; each device has its own settings. Delete all saved data from Settings at any time.

The healthy-age goal is a personal planning marker, not medical advice, diagnosis, or a prediction of your health or lifespan. Activity hours count down to the schedules you set; they do not track attendance or report to an employer.
