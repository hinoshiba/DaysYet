DaysYet keeps three timelines at the edge of your Mac display. See the time left and elapsed progress for your week, month, year, daily work hours, and important dates while you work.

THREE TIMELINES AT THE EDGE
Choose small progress circles on the left or right, or three thin bars at the top. Hover to expand the black panel and reveal the selected timeline. On either side, the details move up or down to follow the circle you select.

FIT YOUR WORKSPACE
Choose left, right, or top placement and the display to use. Drag a side widget up or down; DaysYet saves its position. Adjust the size from 80–150% and choose from four color palettes. Double-click to open settings, where you can also show, hide, or quit the app.

CHOOSE YOUR LEVEL OF DETAIL
Pick a large time-left countdown, a combined view of time left, elapsed percentage, and a bar, or a progress-bar view. In the progress-bar view, choose time left, elapsed percentage, or the end date as the value. Details close when you move away, or you can keep them open from settings.

DAILY WORK HOURS AND YOUR WEEK
Set your work start and end times for a schedule that repeats daily in local time, including overnight shifts. Start This week on the day set by your Mac calendar or choose any weekday.

IMPORTANT DATES AND PERSONAL GOALS
Choose three timelines from this week, this month, this year, work hours, a milestone with its own start date, and a healthy-age goal you set. The default week, month, and year work without entering any personal dates. Japanese and English are supported.

STORED ON THIS MAC
No account is required. Dates, work times, week-start choices, and display settings stay on your Mac and are not sent to the developer. DaysYet has no advertising or analytics SDKs. There is no data sync with iPhone or iPad; each device has its own settings. Delete all saved data from Settings at any time.

The healthy-age goal is a personal planning marker, not medical advice, diagnosis, or a prediction of your health or lifespan. Work hours is a daily countdown, not attendance tracking or employer reporting.
