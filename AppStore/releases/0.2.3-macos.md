Archive, Apple validation, upload, processing, and App Review submission have not been completed for this candidate. This update targets macOS only; iOS remains 0.2.1 (17).

Its approval date and public-store propagation time were not separately established. The [0.2.2 record](0.2.2-macos.md) retains the previous build's source, verification, and submission receipt.

## Changes and source

- Return timing follows travel distance for more consistent movement from short and long distances.
- On edge attachment, the selected circle keeps its size and position while a rounded liquid-like surface spreads along the wall and settles into the final shape.
- The surface stays connected to the wall, and neighboring circles appear whole as space becomes available.
- Existing dates, schedules, colors, display preferences, and cross-display placement are preserved.

The functional changes were merged through [PR #67](https://github.com/hinoshiba/DaysYet/pull/67) as `ad17677`, after [source PR CI](https://github.com/hinoshiba/DaysYet/actions/runs/45290936548) passed. Release preparation began from `761936a`, including the distance-based return, fixed-focus attachment, liquid surface, and rounded-tip refinements. The final merged release commit and its exact-source CI result must be recorded before archiving. No release tag or archive is asserted by this preparation record.

The Mac app, Mac tests, and helper template use 0.2.3 (16). `project.yml`, the generated Xcode project, and `AppStore/macos/configuration.yml` were updated together with the platform-specific version script. The iOS targets and metadata retain 0.2.1 (17).
