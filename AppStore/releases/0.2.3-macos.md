## Changes and source

- Return timing follows travel distance for more consistent movement from short and long distances.
- On edge attachment, the selected circle keeps its size and position while a rounded liquid-like surface spreads along the wall and settles into the final shape.
- The surface stays connected to the wall, and neighboring circles appear whole as space becomes available.
- Existing dates, schedules, colors, display preferences, and cross-display placement are preserved.
