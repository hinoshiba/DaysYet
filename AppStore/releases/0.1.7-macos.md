# 0.1.7 (11) macOS side-detail hover

This candidate updates the native Mac app to 0.1.7 (11). iPhone / iPad stay at
[0.1.7 (11)](0.1.7.md), which is already with App Review; the two platforms now
share a marketing version, as they did at 0.1.6. The existing `v0.1.7-build11`
tag belongs to the iOS candidate and is not reassigned.

## Behavior and review

- At the left and right edges, moving the pointer from an enlarged circle onto
  its details no longer drops the hover. The circle stays magnified and its
  details stay open, including while the pointer crosses the gap between them.
- Moving to another timeline's details moves both the selection and the
  magnification to that circle, and the details follow it.
- Leaving the widget restores the circle's size and closes details that are not
  pinned. With **Keep details open**, returning to the details magnifies the
  selected circle again. A closed surface does not restore a previous hover.
- Top placement is unaffected: it has no graph magnification and takes a
  different hover path.
- Nothing else changed. The iPhone / iPad settings rework in 0.1.7 touches only
  files outside the Mac target, so Mac settings, colors, timelines, study days,
  and activity schedules behave exactly as in 0.1.6 (10).

## Candidate validation

Xcode 26.6 (17F113) passed 194 macOS tests, including three new hover tests that
cover the fix at both side edges: details keep the selected circle magnified
until the pointer leaves, entering pinned details magnifies the selected circle
again, and a closed surface does not restore the previous hover. The 89 iOS unit
tests and four iOS UI tests also pass on the same tree.

Release compliance, including the live support and privacy URL checks, passed.

  magnification in terms this fix makes more reliable, not less accurate.
