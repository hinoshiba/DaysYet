# iOS 0.1.9 (14) resubmission preparation

Status recorded on 2026-10-18: **build 14 is a candidate; archive, upload, and resubmission remain pending.** This is an iPhone / iPad-only replacement. Mac remains 0.1.8 (12).

## Withdrawal and replacement scope

The release owner explicitly requested withdrawal of iOS 0.1.9 (13) and resubmission with [PR #44](https://github.com/hinoshiba/DaysYet/pull/44). On October 18 at 18:22 JST, App Store Connect confirmed **Developer Rejected** for iOS 0.1.9 and made **Add for Review** available. TestFlight listed only build 13 for this version, confirming build 14 was available.

Build 13 had been submitted at 10:43 JST that day. Its [release record](0.1.9.md), source commit `561aaf84df00033c5f29e471272c21ac9e73f5a5`, and immutable [v0.1.9-build13 tag](https://github.com/hinoshiba/DaysYet/tree/v0.1.9-build13) remain historical evidence for that binary. They do not identify the replacement archive. No approval or public availability of 0.1.9 has been confirmed.

Build 14 retains the 0.1.9 unified row/circle editor, in-app circle settings, individual timeline colors, ring labels with contained long names, and improved readability when iOS removes widget backgrounds. PR #44 adds:

- Times and Settings use the inline navigation titles and grouped surfaces already used by Widget.
- Times replaces the large introductory copy with a short instruction. The motto remains in onboarding and About.
- At accessibility text sizes, time cards place titles above values and allow text to wrap, keeping long labels readable.

Navigation and saved settings are unchanged. Lock Screen and StandBy color/background rendering remain controlled by iOS. No new permission, service, dependency, or data collection is introduced by this UI adjustment.

## Source and verification

PR #44 merged as `a6ce39e452b9f124bbd003a5798fed479ab459ab`. The build 14 preparation starts from that commit; the final release source commit, immutable tag, and exact-source unsigned CI still need to be recorded. CI validates the source without signing. Official Archive, Validate, and Upload will use the authorized Mac's Xcode and Organizer.

The following checks passed for PR #44 before the build 14 release preparation:

- Unsigned iOS app/Widget build; 113 iOS tests and 228 Mac tests.
- Compliance and working/index public-file checks; generated project consistency.
- Japanese/English iPhone simulator review at normal and maximum accessibility text sizes in light/dark appearances; Settings navigation/back and Settings → Widget shortcut.
- English iPad Times review and independent source review with no actionable findings.

During build 14 preparation, a fresh unsigned iOS app/Widget build succeeded. Fresh local test runs passed 113 iOS tests and 228 Mac tests with zero failures. The independent static product/version/signing-publication audit found no actionable issues. Release compliance and Store asset validation, including live URLs, passed.

The updated Japanese/English release notes and shared review notes were saved in App Store Connect. Readback matched each trimmed local file; the saved review notes contain 3,877 characters.

All 36 build 14 screenshots passed visual review after the final three recaptures: nine per Japanese/English iPhone/iPad set. The 18 iPhone images are 1320 × 2868 and the 18 iPad images are 2064 × 2752, all RGB without alpha. Settings shows 0.1.9 (14), and all 36 hashes match the regenerated SHA-256 manifest. App Store Connect registration is in progress: the nine English iPhone images are registered; the other sets remain pending.

These results establish local candidate and screenshot checks; they do not establish the final release commit, signed binary, or complete App Store Connect registration. Physical-device VoiceOver was not tested. StandBy and Lock Screen behavior have not been newly verified on a physical device for this replacement.
