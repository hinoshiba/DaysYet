## Changes

- Four-circle typography uses a centered single-line title, consistent percentage sizing, proportional rings, and bounded text frames. Long titles truncate inside the circle. Small remaining-time values use the leading unit; dates use a short year and 24-hour clock. Full names and durations remain available to accessibility.
- The in-app preview uses the actual compact circle view. Settings returns to the selected widget editor, and the last editor is remembered locally and cleared by Delete all data.
- Colors & palette is directly below the preview, alongside the visible Home Screen add/switch guide.
- Long-press a Times card and choose Change color to edit that timeline's color. Its preview updates immediately; normal taps retain schedule/date editing. These are the Times and Three timelines colors; existing circle-specific colors and inheritance are preserved.

The [design review](../../docs/FOUR_CIRCLE_DESIGN_REVIEW.md) records the decisions, alternatives, primary references, and review limitations. Existing widget kinds, profiles, and explicit per-widget settings remain compatible. The app-only editor preference has the required privacy declaration; no networking, permissions, or data collection are added.
