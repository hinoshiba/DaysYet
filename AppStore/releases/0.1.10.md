# iOS 0.1.10 (15)

Release candidate prepared on 2026-10-20. App Store Connect showed iOS 0.1.9 (14) Ready for Distribution and build 14 as the newest iOS upload. This update uses a new marketing version and build; the distributed version does not need withdrawal. Mac remains 0.1.8 (12).

## Changes

- Four-circle typography uses a centered single-line title, consistent percentage sizing, proportional rings, and bounded text frames. Long titles truncate inside the circle. Small remaining-time values use the leading unit; dates use a short year and 24-hour clock. Full names and durations remain available to accessibility.
- The in-app preview uses the actual compact circle view. Settings returns to the selected widget editor, and the last editor is remembered locally and cleared by Delete all data.
- Colors & palette is directly below the preview, alongside the visible Home Screen add/switch guide.
- Long-press a Times card and choose Change color to edit that timeline's color. Its preview updates immediately; normal taps retain schedule/date editing. These are the Times and Three timelines colors; existing circle-specific colors and inheritance are preserved.

The [design review](../../docs/FOUR_CIRCLE_DESIGN_REVIEW.md) records the decisions, alternatives, primary references, and review limitations. Existing widget kinds, profiles, and explicit per-widget settings remain compatible. The app-only editor preference has the required privacy declaration; no networking, permissions, or data collection are added.

## Release verification

Source review, candidate tests, Store material verification, exact-release-commit CI, Archive, Validate, upload, processing, and final submission are tracked separately. A successful source build or merged PR alone does not establish App Store submission or public availability.

Local candidate validation passed on October 20 with zero failures:

- 127 iOS tests, including native circle render attachments, geometry checks, and independent percentage glyph measurements.
- 230 Mac regression tests.
- Eight iPhone UI tests: seven WidgetNavigation tests and the static-card interaction test. These cover Settings returning to the selected editor, the Japanese add/switch guide, selection persistence and reset, screenshot isolation, maximum accessibility text, native color selection/reset, and normal editable-card taps after long-press color editing. Cards without their own settings retain their non-navigating tap behavior.

Independent review of the Times color shortcut and color navigation found no must-fix issue. Store material verification, the final source commit and immutable tag, exact-release-commit CI, Archive, Validate, upload, processing, and final submission remain to be completed and recorded. Automatic release after approval is the intended release method.

Physical-device VoiceOver, Home Screen hosting, StandBy, and Lock Screen behavior have not been newly exercised for this update; native shared-view renders and simulator interaction tests do not establish those physical-device results.
