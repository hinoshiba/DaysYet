# 0.1.7 (11) per-time settings

This candidate updates iPhone / iPad and its Widget extension to 0.1.7 (11).
The native Mac app stays at [0.1.6 (10)](0.1.6-countdown.md); nothing in the Mac
settings or widget changed. The existing `v0.1.6-build10` tag belongs to the
previous candidate and is not reassigned.

## Behavior and review

- Settings no longer offers a combined **Time reference points** screen. The
  settings that belong to a single time moved behind that time's card in the
  Times tab: the two activity schedules, study days, the milestone, and the
  healthy-age goal with its birth date.
- Those five cards carry a chevron and a VoiceOver hint, open a screen titled
  with the time's own name, and hide the tab bar while open. Study days is now
  one tap from Times instead of two taps from Settings.
- This week, this month, and this year have no settings of their own, so their
  cards stay non-interactive and carry no chevron.
- Settings keeps the week start, the one choice several times share: it defines
  the boundaries of "This week" and orders the weekdays in the study-day editor.
  Its footer now says so. Settings is week start, privacy, and about, and fits
  on one screen without scrolling.
- Stored profiles are untouched. Dates, activity schedules, study plans, widget
  overrides, themes, and display-mode values are read and written exactly as
  before, so an upgrade preserves every saved setting.

## Candidate validation

Xcode 26.6 (17F113) passed 89 iOS tests, four iOS UI tests, and 191 Mac tests.
The UI tests cover the new route: `testEditorIsReachableFromTimesAndKeepsChanges`
opens the study editor from the Times card, changes a weekday, navigates back,
reopens the card, and asserts the change persisted; the new
`testTimesWithoutOwnSettingsAreNotTappable` asserts that this week, this month,
and this year expose no button.

Each of the five settings screens was walked in the iPhone 17 Pro simulator in
Japanese and English: the card opens its own screen, the back control returns to
Times, and the tab bar stays hidden while editing. Settings was checked in both
languages for the removed section and for text that fits without truncation.

Release compliance, including the live support and privacy URL checks, passed.
